## Compliance Justification

The obligation requires the enterprise to provide international mutual assistance in the enforcement of personal data protection legislation, encompassing notification, complaint referral, investigative assistance and information exchange, subject to appropriate safeguards for the protection of personal data and other fundamental rights and freedoms. [ref: OBLIGATION]

The enterprise has established a Personal Data Breach Notification Procedure that triggers notifications to supervisory authorities; however, the current procedure does not include a specified coordination mechanism with foreign supervisory authorities for cross‑border investigations, indicating a compliance gap with the mutual assistance requirement. [ref: Personal Data Breach Notification Procedure] Additionally, the Third‑Party / Vendor Risk Assessment evaluates vendors' compliance with cross‑border data transfer regulations, but currently does not address coordination with overseas supervisory bodies. [ref: Third-Party / Vendor Risk Assessment] The Encryption at Rest Standard protects personal data during storage and transfer, supporting the safeguards aspect of the obligation. [ref: Encryption at Rest Standard]

Earlier audit findings, such as EXT-2023-DPC-META and EXT-2022-CALAG-SEPHORA, highlighted analogous deficiencies in cross‑border notification mechanisms and vendor risk assessments, underscoring the need for improvement in this area. [ref: EXT-2023-DPC-META] [ref: EXT-2022-CALAG-SEPHORA]

Open items requiring follow‑up include the development of a formal cross‑border notification and referral procedure and the identification of appropriate safeguards for information exchange; further assessment is required. [ref: INFERRED]
